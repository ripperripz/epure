# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
An editorial bespoke atelier website showcasing watches, rings, and leather watch straps. The supplied brief requires Home, Product Gallery, About Us, and Contact, with product detail/zoom interactions and enquiry paths.

## Users
Inferred from the brief: people exploring a personal commission or a distinctive watch, ring, or strap, who need to understand the visual direction before making an enquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Modify the existing buildless HTML/CSS/JavaScript project in place. Retain the original locally hosted Sunlight and Theinhardt fonts, Bootstrap navigation and Splide runtime. The user subsequently explicitly requested GSAP and Lenis: locally hosted GSAP/ScrollTrigger/SplitText and Lenis now coordinate the editorial motion, with the original observer behavior available as fallback. No framework migration. No cart, payment handling, or internal checkout. Real external purchase links only when supplied. Never imply a form was submitted without a supported endpoint.

## Brand Commitments
The user explicitly pins the JCBiver editorial art direction, premium monochrome treatment, product scale, and restraint, with layered, subtle motion inspired by the Cartier case study. They authorize independent design decisions and immediate implementation.

## Evidence on Hand
Only the existing homepage clone and the user's task brief. No real client brand, client photography, product inventory, prices, specifications, purchase URLs, social handles, contact email, location, history, or form endpoint were supplied. ÉPURE is a temporary working identity; all generated photographs and named product studies are development concepts, not client inventory or real workshop evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, semantic markup, accessible contrast, mobile/tablet responsiveness, and reduced-motion support are required.
